## Logic puzzles

### E11. 25 horses · ⏱ 8 min
> "25 horses, a track that races 5 at a time, and no stopwatch. What's the fewest races to find the top 3?"

### E12. Two eggs · ⏱ 8 min
> "You have 2 eggs and a 100-floor building. What's the fewest drops that guarantee you find the highest
> floor an egg survives?"

### E13. 12 coins · ⏱ 10 min
> "12 coins, one counterfeit, which is either heavier or lighter. You have a balance scale. What's the
> fewest weighings to find it and say whether it's heavy or light?"

### E14. Bridge and torch · ⏱ 5 min
> "Four people cross a bridge at night with one torch. At most two can cross at once, at the slower
> person's pace. Their times are 1, 2, 5, 10 minutes. What's the fastest crossing?"

### E15. Burning ropes · ⏱ 5 min
> "Two ropes each burn in exactly 60 minutes, but unevenly. How do you measure 45 minutes?"

### E16. 100 lockers · ⏱ 5 min
> "100 closed lockers. Person k toggles every k-th locker, for k = 1 … 100. How many are open at the end?"

### E17. Ants on a stick · ⏱ 5 min
> "Ants are placed on a 1 m stick, each facing a random direction, walking at 1 m/min. When two meet,
> both turn around. What's the longest it can take for all of them to fall off?"

### E18. Pirate gold · ⏱ 10 min
> "5 pirates ranked by seniority split 100 gold coins. The most senior proposes a split and everyone
> votes. If at least half vote yes, it passes. Otherwise the proposer is thrown overboard and the next
> most senior proposes. What should the most senior pirate propose?"
